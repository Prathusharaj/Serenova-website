The Serenova Journey

Four stages. One journey.

Serenova combines guided conversations, meaningful human connection, community service and rewarding experiences.

STAGE 01
🧠

Talk & Understand

Begin with guided conversations with a qualified therapist or mental-health professional to understand your concerns and work towards healthier coping strategies.

STAGE 02
🤝

Human Connection

A structured companionship experience where members can request a suitable same-gender or opposite-gender companion, subject to availability, safety and programme rules.

STAGE 03
💚

Give Back

Participate in supervised community-service experiences with organisations such as orphanages or old-age homes.

STAGE 04
🌏

Explore India

Eligible members can unlock curated travel experiences after completing the required participation milestones.

Travel Eligibility

How the milestones work

Progress is about participation, not simply the passage of time.

🌴 Goa milestone

  • Approximately 8–9 months of active participation.
  • Membership payments must be up to date.
  • Required programme participation must be completed.
  • Travel is subject to availability and final approval.

🌿 10-month milestone

  • 10 months of active participation.
  • Completion of required community-service activities.
  • Membership must remain active.
  • Travel dates and destination depend on availability.

💰 Budget transparency

The displayed travel amounts are indicative programme budgets, not guaranteed cash values. Final inclusions, transportation, accommodation, meals and other expenses should be communicated to members before travel.

🛡️ Safety first

All experiences should follow appropriate safety, consent, supervision and safeguarding procedures. Serenova should confirm eligibility and travel arrangements before making any commitment to a member.
